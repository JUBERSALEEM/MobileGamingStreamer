MOBILE GAMING STREAMER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Developer: Juber Saleem
App: Mobile Gaming Streamer (Glip)

This policy describes the current development build. It must be checked again against the exact APK, bundled SDKs and distribution configuration before any public release.

1. Information the app handles

  • Screen video: Android MediaProjection captures the screen or app window that you explicitly select after Android displays its system screen-sharing consent prompt.
  • Microphone audio: when you choose a microphone-containing audio mode and grant Android's microphone permission, the app captures microphone input. This can include your voice and nearby sounds.
  • Internal playback audio: when you choose GAME or a mixed audio mode, the app requests Android playback capture. Android, the device manufacturer and the source app/game can restrict capture. Teammate voice chat is not guaranteed to be included.
  • RTMP destination and stream key: the server address and key you enter are used to connect to your selected streaming endpoint. If “Remember key securely on this device” is enabled, the app encrypts the key using AES-GCM with a non-exportable AES key held by Android Keystore; only the encrypted payload is stored in app-private preferences. If the option is disabled, the app clears the saved encrypted value and does not intentionally persist the key. The key is also present in app memory while needed for the session.
  • Optional floating controls: if you grant Display over other apps permission, the app can show its movable control bubble and panel over other apps. The overlay is marked secure to request exclusion from screen capture; actual behaviour can vary by Android/OEM implementation.
  • Optional YouTube chat: if you enter a live video ID and open chat, an embedded WebView loads YouTube Live Chat. Google/YouTube may process information under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Local preferences: the app stores interface settings such as the selected theme and whether secure stream-key remembrance is enabled.

2. How streaming works

When you initiate a broadcast, the selected screen video and selected audio are sent over the network to the RTMP/RTMPS address you configured. That endpoint and the streaming platform may receive, process, relay, record or publish the stream under their own settings and policies. Use RTMPS when your provider supports it. Plain RTMP does not encrypt stream traffic in transit.

3. Developer collection and third parties

This development build does not require a Mobile Gaming Streamer account and does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics or a developer cloud account backend. The developer does not receive a separate copy of your stream through a Mobile Gaming Streamer server. Your chosen streaming provider receives the broadcast. YouTube content loaded in the optional chat WebView is subject to Google's own processing and policies. The app uses the RootEncoder library for streaming; review the exact dependency and its licence notices for the release build.

4. Permissions and their purposes

  • RECORD_AUDIO: microphone capture and Android playback-capture eligibility for the selected audio mode.
  • MediaProjection consent: screen/app video and eligible internal playback audio for a broadcast you initiate.
  • Foreground service permissions and types: keep an explicitly started screen broadcast operating with an ongoing Android notification.
  • Display over other apps: optional floating controls.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show the ongoing broadcast notification and its stop action where Android requires notification permission.
  • INTERNET: connect to the RTMP/RTMPS endpoint and load optional YouTube Live Chat.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: support the local speaker-volume control.

No AccessibilityService is declared in the current Android manifest. The app does not use an AccessibilityService in this build.

5. Sharing and retention

Audiovisual content is transmitted to the destination you choose. The destination provider controls its own retention, access, publication and deletion practices. The app does not operate a separate stream archive or developer analytics backend in this build. Android may retain system diagnostics according to device settings. The encrypted stream key and local preferences remain in app-private storage until cleared by the app, Android data clearing, or uninstall.

6. Security

Keep your stream key confidential. Secure local storage is optional and uses Android Keystore-backed encryption, but it does not protect a key from someone who can use your unlocked device or the running app. Prefer RTMPS where available. Anyone who obtains your stream key may be able to broadcast to your channel. Avoid showing passwords, personal data or other sensitive content on screen or in voice chat.

7. Children

This app is not designed specifically for children. Parents and guardians should supervise minors' streaming, microphone use and sharing of personal information.

8. Your choices

You can end a broadcast from the app, floating panel or Android notification. You can revoke microphone, notification and overlay permissions in Android settings. You can turn off “Remember key securely on this device” to remove the saved encrypted stream key. Uninstalling the app removes its app-private data, subject to Android behaviour.

9. Changes and release disclosures

This policy will be updated if app functionality, dependencies, destinations or data practices change. Before public distribution, the developer must review the exact release artifact, all bundled SDKs, open-source notices, store Data safety declarations and applicable platform policies.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or support, use the project issue tracker.
